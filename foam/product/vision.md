# Vision & positioning

#product

## What this product is

Open-source Android call filter for **call-center number ranges** and **spam**, with a **self-hosted multi-user backend**, default FR call-center lists, moderated community proposals, temporary or permanent blocks, and analytics that power better suggestions.

## Audience

- **Primary users**: people tired of sequential number blocking for call centers / spam.
- **Operators / admins**: anyone running an instance (home server, family, small community) who moderates proposals and configures retention.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Being a full default dialer / SMS app.
- Worldwide default blocklists on day one.
- Auto-publishing unmoderated community reports.
- Proprietary cloud as the only option.
